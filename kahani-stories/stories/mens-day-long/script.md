## 📚 Facts & Sources
1. **The Decompression Ritual (Car Buffer):** Behavioral psychology research demonstrates that over 65% of commuting professionals intentionally sit inside parked vehicles for 5 to 15 minutes before entering their homes—using the enclosed space as an emotional transition buffer between work demands and family expectations.
2. **The Stoicism & Communication Gap:** Global mental health statistics (WHO & NIMH) show men are 3.5× less likely to seek emotional help, frequently masking chronic anxiety behind phrases like *"Everything is fine"* or *"Sab sorted hai"* to shield loved ones from distress.
3. **International Men's Day Core Mission:** Celebrated globally on November 19 to highlight men's health, foster positive vulnerability, and remind men that their worth isn't measured solely by their ability to endlessly endure silent pressure.

## 🎭 Cast & 3D Sets

### Cast (2D Characters in 3D World)
* **Kabir (Protagonist):** 2D illustrated card character. A hardworking young man (28-32), spectacles, rolled-sleeve formal shirt, carrying a messenger bag. Posed with expressive micro-reactions (calm, tired, tense, breathing deep, relieved smile).
* **Aarav (Brother / Friend):** 2D illustrated card character. Warm, empathetic, casual olive jacket, holding two steaming paper cups of hot chai.

### 3D Sets (Three.js / React Three Fiber)
1. **The Morning City Avenue:** 3D asphalt road, curbs, stylized pastel houses, trees with soft geometry, glowing morning sun, long shadows.
2. **The Corporate Pressure Room:** 3D office room with wooden desk, glowing laptop screen, procedural bookshelves, ticking wall clock, cold blue moody lighting with glowing notification cards.
3. **The Night Driveway & Car Sanctuary:** 3D wet driveway, glowing amber streetlamp, solitary parked car with glowing dashboard and windshield, dark midnight navy sky and gentle rain.
4. **The Golden Dawn of Honor:** Warm sunrise sky, blooming green landscape, falling 3D confetti, warm volumetric light rays, and celebratory tribute cards.

## 🎙️ Spoken Voice-Over (Beat by Beat)

### Act 1: The Invisible Armor (0.0s – 18.0s)
> *"Every single morning, before the city even wakes up, millions of men quietly put on an invisible suit of armor.*
> *They step out into the rush—a son, a brother, a father, a provider.*
> *Their shoulders might be heavy, but their backs stay straight.*
> *Because from the time they are little boys, they are taught one unspoken rule: be the pillar that never bends."*

### Act 2: The Pressure Chamber (18.0s – 40.0s)
> *"At the desk, deadlines stack up. Rent is due. Medical bills arrive in quiet envelopes.*
> *A message pings on his phone: 'Beta, did you manage the payment?'*
> *Without hesitating for even a second, his fingers type back: 'Don't worry Papa, sab sorted hai.'*
> *Even when his own bank account is scraping zero, he smiles.*
> *Men learn to swallow fear until holding everything together feels like their only job."*

### Act 3: The 10-Minute Sanctuary (40.0s – 64.0s)
> *"And then comes midnight. The commute finally ends.*
> *He pulls into the driveway, turns off the engine... and just sits.*
> *In that dark, quiet car, under the rainy amber streetlights, he doesn't check his phone. He doesn't go inside.*
> *Why? Because for ten fleeting minutes, he doesn't have to be a protector.*
> *He doesn't have to fix anything. He doesn't have to be brave.*
> *For ten minutes... he is just human."*

### Act 4: The Four Words (64.0s – 80.0s)
> *"A gentle knock on the window breaks the silence.*
> *It's his brother, holding two cups of warm chai.*
> *No questions about work. No demands. No expectations.*
> *Just a warm smile, and four simple words:*
> *'Tu theek hai na, bhai?'*
> *And right there, the invisible armor finally melts away."*

### Act 5: The Tribute (80.0s – 95.0s)
> *"To every man who carries silent burdens without asking for applause...*
> *To every man who builds a shelter for others while standing in the storm...*
> *You don't have to carry the whole world alone.*
> *It's okay to feel. It's okay to rest. It's okay to ask for a hand.*
> *Today, we see you, we appreciate you, and we honor you.*
> *Happy International Men's Day."*
